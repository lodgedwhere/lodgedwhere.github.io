# Reality is that which cannot be denied.

Every English statement can be denied from the standpoint of another language. Every concept can be denied from outside the framework that gives it meaning. Every distinction can be denied by stepping beyond the boundary that defines it.  
Whatever is defined by what it is not depends upon a context that can itself be questioned. No concept is self-grounding.

But there is one thing that remains prior to every framework: the bare fact that experience is present. One may dispute what is seen, how it should be interpreted, or even whether there is a separate self having it. Yet the actuality of experience itself cannot be denied, because every denial already appears within it.

The world described by thought is always vulnerable to a larger context. The fact of experience is not. It is the condition under which every affirmation and every denial becomes possible
